About

Recipes have a way of creating beautiful memories; memories of times together in the kitchen (or basement!), of incredible food at a family gathering, or memories from childhood of something special that was made. Recipes create memories and feelings of love; they are the stories that have been passed down from generation to generation. These meals create ties that bind us together as family, uphold traditions, and remind us of who we are, and where we have come from.

In honor of our Nonna, Assunta Nesci, we have created this site to share special dishes that we hold in our hearts. Nonna makes a cheesecake that is love. We don’t want to lose this recipe - we want this to be here eternally, just as Nonna’s love is always with us.

We encourage Nonnos and Nonnas, Zias and Zios, (and cousins!) to send over the recipes that hold a special place in their heart. Click here if you want to submit a recipe. Hopefully we can bring everyone together, and keep our heritage living on.

Dove c’è amore, c’è cibo. - Where there is love, there is food.
